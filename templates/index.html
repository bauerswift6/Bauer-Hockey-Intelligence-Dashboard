        Team Cap Efficiency

        Total Surplus Value rolled up across each team's matched contracts. Teams with high total Surplus Value have more cap flexibility to make trades and sign additional players. Aggregated only over contracts in the curated dataset (~70 highest cap hits + bargains).

        # Team Players Counted Total Cap Total GAR Avg GAR/$1M Total Surplus

        How to use this

        Surplus Value is the headline metric here: it's what a player produces in goals, translated to dollars at the open-market rate of $1.85M per GAR, minus their actual cap hit. Positive means team-friendly, negative means overpaid. Sustainability Score (GAR − xGAR) flags players running hot or cold versus their underlying play. The contract type badges keep apples-to-apples comparisons honest — ELC players are structurally underpaid, not 'best-value signings'. For stat definitions, see the Glossary page.
